## Changelog

- Redesigned the Command Center with textured steel frames, crimson controls, and clearer shortcuts.
- Updated the Bounty Board, Calls to Arms, Settings Center, and other screens with a consistent layout and clearer controls.
- Added an Alert Command Center for previewing and positioning Guild Death and Bounty Spotted alerts and opening their settings.
- Improved readability in the PvP Leaderboard and Raid Attendance.
- Retired achievements and commendations from active use. Existing historical points remain in SavedVariables.
- Open Keepers War Command with /kwc, /kt, or /keepers.

This alpha targets WoW Forever 1.60.1. The updated layouts still need broader in-game testing across window sizes and UI scales.
