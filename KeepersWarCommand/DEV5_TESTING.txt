Commands:
/kt autosync           show status
/kt autosync on/off    toggle automatic sync
/kt sync               force manual unified request (subject to cooldown)

record, verify the other client's state without pressing Sync. Then log one
client out and back in and verify reconciliation. Test honor leaderboard after
