# Raid Tools attribution

Keepers War Command's Raid Tools incorporate work from Purplexity Raid Tools
by nivthefox.

Original project: https://www.wowinterface.com/downloads/info27017-v1.4.0.html

# Trogdor artwork

The Trogdor emblem is the Keepers of Trogdor owner's own artwork. KWC 1.x
illustrated media (Media/*.tga from the WarCommand addon) and 1.x alert
artwork are NOT shipped in 2.0. Fonts used (FRIZQT, ARIALN, MORPHEUS, SKURRI)
ship with the game client and are not bundled.

# Bundled library notices

- AceComm-3.0 and CallbackHandler-1.0 are embedded components. See the Ace3 license text in each library folder.
- ChatThrottleLib and LibStub retain their public-domain notices in source.
- LibSerialize and LibDeflate retain their license notices in source.
- LibSharedMedia-3.0 identifies LGPL 2.1 in its header. Its source and a copy of the license are included.

KWC 2.0 additions
-----------------
- Media/Emblem.tga (War Room) and KeepersWarCommand_Command/Media/Seal.tga
  (Command Table) are refinements of the guild-owned Trogdor emblem previously
  shipped as Textures/TrogdorMinimap.tga in KWC 1.x.
- Media/Grain.tga and Media/Crimson.tga are procedurally generated for 2.0.
- No legacy KWC 1.x interface textures are included in 2.0.
